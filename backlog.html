Scores come from this site's catalogues where a title is matched — verified Metascores for games, films and series, compiled ratings for books. Where a title is not in the catalogue, the ranking falls back to the rating in your own export and says so. Lengths are runtime for film, an estimate of ten episodes a season for series, and your export's page count at roughly a page a minute for books; games have no reliable length source, so they are ranked on quality alone. Fan-made tool, not affiliated with Goodreads, Letterboxd, IMDb or Metacritic.
