How it works

Gathered, scored, then checked

Three passes stand between a public listing and a card on these shelves.

1

Gather

Titles are collected from public sources across every genre, era and niche — blockbusters and deep cuts alike.

2

Score

Each entry picks up its critic rating: Metacritic and IGN for games, Goodreads ratings and vote counts for books.

3

Verify & merge

Top scores are re-checked against Metacritic and earn a ✓. Duplicate entries are merged rather than stacked.

About the data — honestly

These catalogues were compiled with AI assistance from public sources. Scores marked ✓ were checked against Metacritic directly. Everything else — IGN numbers, Goodreads ratings, release years, blurbs — is a close approximation and may be wrong in the details. Shelf is a discovery tool, not a citation source. Cover art belongs to its respective publishers.
