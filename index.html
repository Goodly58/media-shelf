How it works

1

Gather

We sweep public sources for notable titles across every genre, era and niche — blockbusters and deep cuts alike.

2

Score

Each entry gets its critic rating: Metacritic and IGN for games, Goodreads ratings and vote counts for books.

3

Verify & merge

Top scores are checked against Metacritic directly and earn a ✓. Duplicates merge instead of piling up.

About the data — honestly

Catalogues were compiled with AI assistance from public sources. Scores carrying a ✓ were verified against Metacritic; the rest, including IGN and Goodreads figures, are close approximations. A discovery tool, not a citation source. Cover art © its respective publishers.
